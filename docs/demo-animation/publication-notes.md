## Practical publishing guidance

Ask Igloo and the credited creator to identify the appropriate rights holder and
obtain written permission covering the specific code/assets, modification, live
portfolio hosting, source sharing and any intended promotional posts. Alternatively,
replace the reused implementation and assets with original or suitably licensed work.
Copyright generally protects expression rather than the underlying idea.

A tag, link, educational label, no-affiliation disclaimer or promise to remove on
request does not itself authorize copying. Exceptions vary by jurisdiction and
circumstances; a public portfolio or social post is not automatically exempt.
For a public launch, an IP professional can assess the actual materials and use.

Be precise about your contribution. A suggested truthful disclosure, if the use
is otherwise authorized, is:

> Technical study adapting the Igloo.inc 3D experience (original site credits
> Abeto), including original rendering code and assets. React integration,
> custom project UI and portfolio projects by David Okoye. Unaffiliated with
> Igloo Inc. or Abeto.

Do not claim permission or endorsement unless actually granted. Keep applicable
copyright and license notices, and follow any additional attribution conditions.
The existing Unfold credit relates to the mountain experiment and is not an Igloo
reuse license or a substitute for the separate 3D attribution.
